Delete your Halal Table account

Halal Table is operated by Shahreare Khan, an individual based in California, United States, doing business as Saltbridge Studio. Use this page to ask us to delete your Halal Table account and the data tied to it. You do not need the app, and you do not need to be signed in.

First, cancel any subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription you bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google bills you, not us, so the charges go on until you cancel in the store. Cancel first:

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, or go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

If you have already deleted your account, you can still cancel the same way, because the subscription belongs to your Apple or Google account. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Delete it in the app

Open Halal Table, go to Profile, and choose Delete account at the bottom. Confirm, and your account is deleted at once.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, email support@halaltable.app with the subject "Delete my Halal Table account".

  • Write from the email address you signed up with, and give your username.
  • If you no longer have that address, write from another one. Before we delete anything we will ask you to confirm something only the account's owner would know, such as a review you wrote.
  • We reply within a few days, and your account is deleted within 30 days of your request.

Deleting an account cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with the same email address, but you start with an empty profile.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: your email address, username, display name, avatar and sign-in.
  • The content posted under it: reviews, photos, replies, votes, badges, saved places and your block list.
  • If you manage a place: your business claims, and what you posted as its owner. You give up every place you manage.

What we keep

  • Places you added stay listed, without your name.
  • Changes you made to a place's details (as its owner) stay with the place, without your name.
  • Support purchase records are kept without the link to your account, because the app stores' rules and tax rules require a record of payments.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period and then discarded.
  • Backups. We keep encrypted nightly backups of the database and photos for 30 days, so the service can be recovered after an accident. Backups do not include sign-in sessions. A deleted account's data can stay in those backups until they expire, and is then gone for good.
  • Reported content. Content that is the subject of an open report or a legal hold may be kept until the matter is closed.

The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and who we share it with, and the Support page answers other questions. You can also write to us at support@halaltable.app.
