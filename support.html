Halal Table — Support

Halal Table is a community directory of halal restaurants, markets, bakeries and caterers, made by one developer in California. If something is wrong or you need help, email cacti_paws.1d@icloud.com. We answer within a few days, and reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.

A listing is wrong or missing

Open the place in the app and tap Report to tell us what is wrong, or email us the name and address. To add a place that is missing, use the Add tab.

I own or manage a listed business

Email us to correct your listing, to record your certification (we will ask to see the certificate), or to remove your listing. Members' honest reviews of your business are their own and are not removed at a business's request.

How are places verified?

Every place carries one of four labels:

  • Certified — a moderator has seen a certificate from a named certifying body.
  • Community confirmed — enough members have reported first-hand that the place is halal.
  • Unverified — listed, but nobody has confirmed it yet. Most listings start here.
  • Disputed — members disagree, or a report is open.

Halal Table is not a certifier. Labels reflect documents we have been shown and what members report. Use your own judgement and ask the business when in doubt.

Someone posted something objectionable

Every review, photo and reply has a report control. We act on reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the rules, and suspend the accounts that post it.

Managing or cancelling Supporter

Supporter subscriptions and tips are billed by Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Support unlocks nothing functional; it only adds a badge beside your name.

Deleting my account

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your profile and the content you posted, as described in the Privacy Policy. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Halal Table collects only what the app needs: your email address to sign in, the content you contribute, and your location while the app is open so it can show what is nearby. There is no advertising and no tracking.
