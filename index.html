How we verify كيف نتحقق

Four labels, always stated

A halal claim is only as good as the reason behind it. Every listing carries exactly one of these labels, with the evidence one tap away.

Certified halal|حلال معتمد

A moderator has seen a current certificate from a named certifying body. The certifier and a link to its record are on the place page.

Community confirmed|مؤكد من المجتمع

At least three more members have confirmed it than disputed it, each saying how they know: they ate there, saw a certificate, or were told by staff.

Not yet verified|غير مؤكد

Listed as halal, but nobody has confirmed it yet. Most listings start here, and you can be the one who checks.

Disputed|محل خلاف

Members disagree about whether the place is halal. Read what each side says and decide for yourself.

Halal Table is not a certifier. Labels reflect the documents moderators have seen and what members report. When in doubt, ask the business.

Our promises وعودنا

Trust is never for sale

No paid placement

No one can buy a halal label or a higher place in the list. Certified means a moderator saw the certificate, nothing else. Members can support the app for a Supporter badge beside their name; it never changes a label, a ranking or moderation.

No ads, no trackers

No advertising and no analytics or advertising trackers. Your location is used for the nearby search and is not stored. Privacy Policy.

Run with the community

Every review, photo and reply can be reported, and you can block anyone. We act on reports within 24 hours.
