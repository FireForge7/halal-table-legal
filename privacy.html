Privacy Policy

Halal Table — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Halal Table is operated by Shahreare Khan, an individual based in California, United States, doing business as Saltbridge Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Halal Table app, why, who sees it, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read.

Contact: support@halaltable.app

The short version

  • We collect what you give us: an email address, a username, and whatever you choose to post.
  • Your location is used on your phone and in a single search request to find places near you. We do not store it.
  • We show no ads, run no advertising or analytics trackers, and never sell your data. We count how often each place's page is opened, as numbers only (see "Place statistics").
  • Payments go through Apple on iPhone and Google Play on Android. We never see your card.
  • Reviews, photos and replies are public, with your username next to them.
  • If you claim a business, only our moderators see your claim. Members see only that the place is managed by its owner, never who you are.
  • You can delete your account, and everything tied to it, from the Profile screen.

What we collect

Account. When you sign up we store your email address, a username, and a password that is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider. You may add a display name and an avatar. Your email is never shown to other members.

Content you post. Reviews and ratings, photos and their captions, replies, confirmation votes with any note, places you submit, places you save, and reports you file. Content is stored with your account id and the time it was posted.

Members you block. If you block another member, we store that block against your account so their content can be hidden from you. It is stored and deleted the same way as any other content tied to your account — see "How long we keep it" below.

Support purchases. If you support Halal Table, Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android) processes the payment and RevenueCat (our subscription service) tells us what happened: the product, price and currency, the dates, and whether it renewed, was cancelled, expired or was refunded. We store those events against your account so your badge can be shown and your support history displayed to you. We do not receive your card details, billing address, Apple ID or Google account. The same applies to a Halal Table for Business plan if you buy one, and we also store which of your places it covers.

Business claims. If you claim a place as its owner or manager, we store the claim with your account: the place, how you asked to be checked (a call to the number on the listing, an email at the business's own domain, or a document), the contact detail you give (your business email address, or who to ask for and when to call), any note you add, the claim's status (waiting, verified, not verified, withdrawn or ended) and the moderator's note on the decision. Our moderators read all of it, together with your username, when your account was created, and whether you have reviewed or voted on the place. While you manage a place, moderators can also see when you last used Halal Table, because a claim can pass to someone else after six months away. A document you send for a claim reaches us by email, not through the app; we use it only to check the claim and delete it once the claim is decided. While you manage a place, we keep a record of each change you make to its hours, phone number and website, and of each certificate and closure report you send, so a moderator can put a mistaken change right.

Business owners. A verified owner with a Business plan can reply to reviews, post announcements and offers, and add photos and a menu link. These are public and are shown as "from the owner", without the owner's name or username; who manages a place stays private to the owner and our moderators. Owner photos are checked by a moderator before they appear. If the owner's claim ends, their content stops being shown and is kept for moderation. Deleting the owner's account deletes it.

Email you send us. If you email support@halaltable.app, we keep your message and address to answer you and to follow up on what you raise.

Location. When you use the Nearby list or the map, the app reads your device location, with your permission, and sends the coordinates to our server in the search request so it can return places near you. We do not store your location or keep a history of where you have been. You can deny location access; the app then searches around a default area or the map position you choose.

Addresses you type. When you add a place, the address you enter is sent through our server to OpenStreetMap's geocoding service to find its coordinates. The address is a business address, not yours. We keep a record of when your account made a lookup, without the address, to limit how many lookups one account can make.

On your phone only. To decide when to show the store's "rate this app" prompt (Apple's on iPhone, Google Play's on Android), the app keeps a few counts on your phone: how many times you have opened it, and whether you have opened a place or confirmed one. They never leave your phone and are deleted with the app. The rating itself goes to Apple or Google Play, and nothing is ever given in return for one.

Our website. halaltable.app has no cookies, ads or trackers. A place's page (the link the app's Share button sends) reads that place's current halal label from our database when it opens, so the label is never out of date; like any web request, it reaches our hosting provider with your IP address, and we store nothing from it.

Technical data. Our hosting provider records ordinary server logs (such as IP address, request time and device type) for security and to keep the service running. RevenueCat's software in the app records a device identifier and app version so purchases can be matched to your device. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not fingerprint devices. We count place-page views and taps ourselves, as described under "Place statistics".

Place statistics. When a place's page is opened, or someone taps its directions, phone number or website, the app adds one to a count for that place and day. Each count is a number only. The app sends it without your sign-in, and the counts do not record who opened a page, tapped or saved, or anything about your device. When a member saves a place, we add one to its saves. A page opened several times in one session counts once. Your own list of saved places is kept with your account (see "Content you post"). The verified owner of a place can see its monthly totals while it has a Business plan. The counts are never used to rank places.

Why we use it

  • To run the service: show listings, publish your content, count confirmation votes, award points and badges.
  • To moderate: review reported content, hold content that trips the profanity filter, and act on abuse.
  • To check business claims, decide between competing claims, and undo a mistaken change to a listing.
  • To show your support status and history.
  • To protect the service from abuse, and to comply with the law.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent it.

What is public

Your username, display name, avatar, level, badges (including Supporter, Founding Supporter and Backer), reviews, ratings, photos, replies and the fact that you confirmed a place are visible to everyone who uses the app, including people who are not signed in. Your email address, support history, reports, saved places and the members you have blocked are private to you and to us.

If you manage a place, the public signs of it are a "Managed by the owner" line on the place's page and the replies, announcements, offers and photos you post as its owner, which are shown as "from the owner", without your name or username. Who manages it, your claim, its contact details, note and status are private to you and to us. The hours, phone number, website and menu link you set are public, as part of the listing.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, each of which processes data only to provide its service to us:

  • Supabase — hosting, database, authentication and photo storage. Servers in the United States.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management. Receives your account id and purchase events from Apple and Google Play.
  • Apple — on iPhone, payment processing for support purchases, and map display through Apple Maps, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Google Play and Google Maps — on Android, payment processing for support purchases through Google Play, and map display through Google Maps. Google receives the requests the map makes, which include the area shown on the map and the device's IP address, under Google's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation — geocoding of business addresses you enter. Receives the address only.
  • GitHub — stores our encrypted nightly database backups (see "How long we keep it"). The backups are encrypted before they reach GitHub, which cannot read them.
  • Cloudflare and Google — Cloudflare receives email sent to support@halaltable.app and forwards it to our mailbox, which Google hosts. This includes any document you email us for a business claim.
  • Resend — sends our account emails, such as the sign-up confirmation, and may send our replies from support@halaltable.app. Receives the recipient's address and the message.

We share personal data otherwise only when the law requires it, to enforce our Terms of Use, or to protect the rights and safety of members, businesses or the public. If Halal Table is ever transferred to a new operator, your data would go with it under this same policy, and you would be told.

How long we keep it

Your account and content stay until you delete them. Deleting your account from the Profile screen removes your profile and the content posted under it — reviews, photos, replies, votes, badges, saved places, your block list and your business claims. It also gives up every place you manage; the record of changes you made to a place's details stays with the place, without your name. Support purchase records tied to a deleted account are kept without the account link, because the app stores' rules and tax rules require a record of payments. Business claims stay with your account until you delete it: a claim that was not verified, or that you withdrew, keeps its contact detail and note, and a claim that ended (you gave the place up, it passed to a new owner, or we ended it) stays marked as ended, with the moderator's note. Server logs are kept for a short period and then discarded. We also keep encrypted nightly backups of the database and photos for 30 days, so the service can be recovered after an accident. Backups do not include sign-in sessions. A deleted account's data can stay in those backups until they expire, and is then gone for good. Content that is the subject of an open report or a legal hold may be kept until the matter is closed.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. Your profile is editable in the app. Ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you at any time.
  • Deletion. Delete your account in the app, ask for it on our account deletion page without the app, or email us.
  • Location. Change location permission in your device settings at any time.
  • Marketing. We send none.

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in that law. If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your supervisory authority; our legal bases are performance of our contract with you (running the service) and our legitimate interests (security and moderation). To exercise any right, email us. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

Halal Table is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, and access to our database is restricted by role so that the app can only read and write what a member is allowed to. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you as the law requires.

International transfers

Halal Table is operated from the United States and our providers store data there. If you use it from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The effective date at the top always shows the current version.
