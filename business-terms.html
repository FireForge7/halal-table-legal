Halal Table — Business Terms

Effective date: November 26, 2026

These Business Terms apply when you claim a listed place as its owner or manager, and for as long as you manage it. They add to our Terms of Use, which still apply to everything you do in Halal Table. Where the two differ on a business matter, these terms decide.

You agree to these terms when you send a claim. If you send it for a business, you confirm that you are allowed to act for that business, and "you" means both you and the business.

Contact: support@halaltable.app

1. Claiming a place

Any business can claim its listing in the app for free. Open the place, choose "Own this place? Claim it", and pick how we should check that you run it:

  • A call to the listed number. A moderator calls a phone number the listing has shown and asks for you. We never call a number you give us in the claim instead.
  • An email at your business's domain. A moderator writes to your address at the domain of a website the listing has shown, and you reply to confirm.
  • A document by email. After you send the claim, you email us a business licence, utility bill or similar document that shows the business name and address.

A moderator checks every claim by hand. We may ask for more, and we may turn down a claim we cannot verify. Verifying a claim confirms only that we were satisfied you manage the place. It is not an endorsement of the business, and it is not a halal certification.

You can withdraw a claim while it waits. One account manages a place at a time.

2. What managing a place gives you, free

While your claim is verified you can:

  • keep the place's opening hours, phone number and website up to date. Your changes go live without review, and we keep a record of each one so a moderator can put it back if it is wrong;
  • send us the place's halal certificate. A moderator checks it against the certifier's own published list before the place is labelled Certified, exactly as for any member's claim;
  • tell us the place has closed for good. A moderator closes the listing;
  • show a small "Managed by the owner" line on the place's page.

Members see only that line. We never show them who you are, your email address or your note.

3. What a claim never does

Members trust Halal Table because no business can buy or edit its standing. So a claim, and any paid plan, never:

  • sets or changes a place's halal label. Only a certifier's own list makes a place Certified, and only members' votes make it Community confirmed or Disputed;
  • removes, hides or reorders reviews, or changes a rating. You can report a review that breaks our rules, like any member, and we judge it by the same rules;
  • moves a place up a list, the map or a search. Ranking is never paid;
  • lets you review, confirm or dispute your own place. A vote you cast on it before your claim stops counting while you manage it, and you cannot change a review or vote you posted on it before;
  • earns points, or carries weight in levels, trust or moderation.

4. Your responsibilities

You agree to:

  • give true information in your claim and keep your listing accurate;
  • use what you learn through Halal Table only to manage your listing. Do not contact, pressure, threaten or pay members about what they post;
  • not offer anyone a reward, discount or anything else for a review, a rating or a vote, and not post or arrange reviews or votes of your own place or of a competitor's;
  • not claim a place you do not run, or pretend to be someone you are not;
  • follow the community rules in our Terms of Use in everything you send us, including notes and certificate details.

The hours, phone number, website and other details you give us become part of the public listing. You give us the same licence to them as to any content under the Terms of Use, and the right to keep showing them after your claim ends, because the listing is a public record of the place.

5. Two claims, a sale, and ending a claim

  • Competing claims. If more than one person claims a place, a moderator decides. The verified owner keeps the place until someone shows a stronger claim.
  • A sale or a new manager. When a business changes hands, the new owner claims the place and a moderator checks it as usual. Once the new claim is verified, the earlier owner's claim ends.
  • Long absence. If you have not used Halal Table for six months and someone else verifies that they run the place, we may end your claim and verify theirs.
  • Giving it up. You can give up a place at any time from its dashboard. Deleting your account gives up every place you manage.
  • Ending it ourselves. We may end a claim, or refuse one, if it turns out to be false, if you break these terms or the Terms of Use, or if the place closes or is removed from the directory. We will tell you why when we can.

When a claim ends, the "Managed by the owner" line goes away. The listing stays, with the details it had. If you disagree with a decision, email us and a person will look at it again.

6. A paid plan, when we offer one

We plan to offer an optional subscription, "Halal Table for Business", with tools for managing a place. Claiming, and everything in section 2, stays free whether or not you subscribe. If we offer the plan:

  • you buy it in the app through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple charges your Apple ID, and the price is shown before you confirm;
  • it renews automatically at the same price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings;
  • it covers one place you manage, which you choose. It can move to another of your places at most once in 30 days;
  • it buys tools, never standing. Section 3 applies to everything it includes;
  • if it lapses, the tools lock and nothing else changes: your claim and the "Managed by the owner" line stay;
  • if your claim ends, your subscription does not cancel itself. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Refunds go through Apple. Apple processes every payment and decides every refund, under Apple's own terms, at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue a refund ourselves. We never see your card details.

The disclaimers and limits on liability in section 9 of the Terms of Use apply to everything in these terms. You are responsible for your business, its food and what you tell customers about it.
